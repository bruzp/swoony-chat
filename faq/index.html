Frequently asked questions

Getting started

What is Swoony?

An anonymous chat site for adults. Pick a nickname, see who's online, and have private one on one chats with new people.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no email, password or sign-up. You choose a nickname and tell us your gender, age and country, and you're in. Your profile disappears when you leave.

Who can use Swoony?

Adults only. You must be 18 or older and confirm it before you start chatting. If you're under 18, please don't use Swoony.

Is it free?

Yes. There's nothing to buy and no paid plan.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Swoony runs in your browser, on your phone or your computer.

Where is Swoony available?

Almost everywhere. It isn't available in the United Kingdom, Australia or Indonesia yet.

What can I use as a nickname?

3 to 20 letters, numbers or underscores. It must not be in use by someone online right now, and it can't contain rude words or contact details.

Chatting

How do I start a chat?

Open the online list, narrow it by gender, age or country if you like, and tap someone to say hi. You can also read their short bio first.

Are there group chats or public rooms?

No. Every chat on Swoony is private, just the two of you.

Can I send photos, links or my number?

No. Photos aren't supported, and links, phone numbers, emails and social media handles are blocked. This keeps chats on Swoony, where block and report work. Emojis are welcome.

Why was my message blocked?

  • It had a link or contact details.
  • It had swearing, sexual words or slurs.
  • It was longer than 1,000 characters.
  • You were sending messages too fast, or the same message to many people.

Will I know when someone messages me?

Yes, you'll see an alert on screen. Swoony never plays sounds.

Can the other person see when I'm typing or have read their message?

Yes. While you type, they see a typing bubble in the chat. Once you've opened the chat and viewed their messages, they see "Seen" under the newest one you've viewed, and it stays there until you view a newer one. The same works for you. This can't be turned off, and it's deleted along with the chat.

Can I add a bio?

Yes, an optional one of up to 150 characters. The same rules as messages apply: no links, contact details or rude words. There are no profile photos.

What if I open Swoony in another tab?

You stay the same person. Swoony runs in one tab at a time; the other tab shows "Swoony is open in another tab" with a button to switch back.

What happens when I leave?

About a minute after you close your last Swoony tab, you're removed from the online list and your profile is deleted. Reloading the page or locking your phone briefly won't log you out. If you're inactive for 30 minutes, you're signed out automatically.

Privacy

Can anyone read my chats?

No one at Swoony reads your chats. Chats disappear from your screen when they end. To comply with Philippine law, we keep basic connection records for 6 months and the messages of all chats for 1 year, encrypted and only for law enforcement.

Can I see my old chats?

No. Chats aren't saved for you. When a chat ends, it's gone from your screen for good.

What does Swoony keep about me?

While you're online: your nickname, profile and the chats you're in. For the law: basic connection records (who chatted with whom, when, IP address and nickname) for 6 months, and the messages of all chats for 1 year. These records are encrypted and are only used for law enforcement. See the Privacy Policy for the full list.

Does Swoony use cookies?

One, to recognise your device. It keeps you as the same person across tabs and makes bans work. Your light or dark mode choice is saved on your device only. Our analytics don't use cookies.

Safety and moderation

How do I block someone?

Open their profile, or the menu in your chat, and tap Block. The chat ends and you won't see each other for the rest of your visit.

How does reporting work?

Tap Report and pick a reason. Reporting also blocks the person and ends the chat, and we keep that chat's messages (encrypted, for up to 1 year) so the authorities can act on serious cases. A report counts toward a ban only if you chatted with that person and they sent you at least one message.

Who decides on bans?

Bans are automatic. When several different people report the same user within a day, that user is banned. Bans start at 1 hour and get longer if it happens again.

Why was I told to slow down, or removed?

Sending messages or starting chats too fast, or sending the same text to many people, gets you a "slow down" warning. Short greetings like "hi" or "hello" are fine to send to anyone. Three warnings in 10 minutes and you're removed. Removed three times in a day, and you're banned for a while.

I think someone is under 18. What should I do?

Stop chatting and report them, choosing "underage" as the reason.

How do I contact Swoony?

Email abuse@swoony.chat, especially for anything involving a child. More tips are on our Safety page.
