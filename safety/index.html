Safety

Block

Open someone's profile, or the menu in a chat, and tap Block. You stop seeing each other for the rest of your visit, and any chat between you ends.

Report

If someone you chatted with breaks the rules, tap Report and choose a reason. Reporting also blocks them and ends the chat. When several different people report the same user, Swoony bans them automatically. We keep the messages of a reported chat (encrypted, for up to 1 year) so the authorities can act on serious cases.

Stay safe

  • Don't share your real name, address, school, workplace or photos.
  • Swoony blocks phone numbers, emails and social handles for your safety. Don't try to get around it.
  • Never send money or gift cards to someone you met here.
  • If someone seems to be under 18, stop chatting and report them.
  • You can leave any chat at any time. You don't owe anyone a reply.

Contact us

Report abuse, especially anything involving a child, to abuse@swoony.chat. Include the nickname and the date and time. If someone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first.

For law enforcement

Official requests (takedown notices, preservation requests, subpoenas and court orders) go to abuse@swoony.chat and must be sent from an official government email address. Please include the legal basis, the nicknames or times concerned, and a contact person. We act on takedown notices within 24 hours and preserve records on request, in line with Republic Act No. 11930.
