Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

No one at Swoony reads your chats. Chats disappear from your screen when they end. To comply with Philippine law, we keep basic connection records for 6 months and the messages of all chats for 1 year, encrypted and only for law enforcement.

1. Who is responsible

Swoony is run by [OPERATOR NAME], Philippines, the personal information controller under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173). Our Data Protection Officer is [DPO NAME], reachable at [DPO EMAIL].

2. What we process

  • Your profile: nickname, gender, age, country and optional bio. Other users see it while you are online. Your age is sensitive personal information under Philippine law; we use it only to show it on your profile and in filters.
  • Your messages: relayed to the person you chat with and kept only while the chat is open so that a dropped connection can catch up. They are deleted from the live chat within 10 minutes after the chat ends and never later than 24 hours, except the encrypted records we keep for the law (section 4).
  • Connection data: your IP address, a random device id, session times, and who you chatted with and when.
  • Safety data: reports, blocks, bans and rate-limit counters. Reports and bans store scrambled (hashed) device and network ids, not your IP address.

3. Why we process it

  • to run the chat you asked for (your consent when you enter);
  • to keep Swoony safe: filters, reports, automatic bans and spam protection;
  • to comply with the law, in particular Republic Act No. 11930 (Anti-OSAEC and Anti-CSAEM Act), which requires us to preserve certain records and to cooperate with the authorities.

4. How long we keep it

What How long
Your profile Until you leave (about 1 minute after your last tab closes, or after 30 minutes without activity)
Chat messages on your screen (the live chat) Until 10 minutes after the chat ends, never more than 24 hours
Messages of all chats, kept apart from the live chat 1 year, encrypted, for law enforcement only
Connection and chat records (nickname, IP address, country, hashed device id, who chatted with whom, when) 6 months, encrypted, for law enforcement only
Messages of a reported chat, or of a chat taken down after a notice from the authorities 1 year from the report or takedown, encrypted, for law enforcement only
Reports and bans (hashed ids) 6 months
Log of each time the owner searches, downloads or acts on records (hashed ids and the request reference, never nicknames, IP addresses or message text) 1 year, or longer while related records are preserved for the authorities

If the authorities ask us to preserve specific records for an investigation, we keep them until the date the request asks for, even past the periods above.

5. Who can see it

Other users see only your public profile and the messages you send them. The retained records can only be opened by the owner of Swoony, and only to answer a lawful request from the authorities (for example the PNP, NBI, a prosecutor or the Department of Justice). We never sell your data and we show no ads based on it.

Service providers that process data for us:

  • Cloudflare: hosting, storage and network security.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile: checks that you are a human when you enter.
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics: counts page visits without cookies and without tracking you across sites.

6. Cookies and storage on your device

  • sw_dev: a signed cookie with a random device id, so all your tabs are the same user and bans work. It lasts up to 1 year.
  • Your theme choice (light or dark) and a cached emoji list are stored in your browser only. The number of the last message you saw in each chat is kept in your browser for this visit, so a reconnecting chat only fetches newer messages.

7. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to be informed, to access, to object, to correct, to erasure or blocking, to data portability, to damages, and to file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission. Because Swoony has no accounts, we can usually only find your records if you tell us your nickname and when you used Swoony. Records we must keep by law can only be erased once the legal retention period ends. To use your rights, contact our Data Protection Officer.

8. Security and breaches

Retained records are encrypted and access is limited to the owner. If a personal data breach happens, we notify the National Privacy Commission and affected users as the law requires, within 72 hours of knowing about it.

9. Children

Swoony is for adults only. If you believe a user is under 18, report them in the chat or email abuse@swoony.chat.

10. Where Swoony is available

Swoony is run from the Philippines. It is not available in the United Kingdom, Australia or Indonesia, and it does not target users in the European Union.
